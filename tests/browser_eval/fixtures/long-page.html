October community meetup

Saturday 18 October, the old tram depot. Doors open at 10:00.

Morning talks

Three short talks on neighbourhood energy co-operatives, a repair café report and the new library hours.

Lunch

Soup and bread from the bakery on Kade Street. Vegan options are available.

Workshops

Bike maintenance, seed swapping and an introduction to the tool library.

Afternoon

A walk along the canal led by the historical society, followed by the open microphone.

Getting there

Tram 4 to the depot stop; bicycle racks are at the north entrance.

Attend

Let us know you are coming so we bake enough bread.
